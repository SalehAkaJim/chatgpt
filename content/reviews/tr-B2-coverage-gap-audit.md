# Turkish B2 coverage and C1-boundary audit

Status: active / incomplete

## Source-of-truth snapshot

This audit is based on current `main` after cumulative Turkish B1 audio closure. B1 is not reopened here. Turkish B2 is the first genuinely incomplete active-scope level.

Current B2 production is a legacy first tranche with 10 production units: nuanced stance, causes/consequences, hypothetical alternatives, formal negotiation, passive processes, relative detail, inference/evidence, concession/disagreement, summarizing viewpoints, and the community-forum capstone. The B2 spec is still a single `batch-01.json`. Existing units are useful and should be preserved where they remain level-appropriate, but this is not enough breadth/recycling for release completion.

## What the existing B2 tranche already does well

- qualified stance and partial agreement
- multi-factor cause/consequence
- unreal/counterfactual alternatives
- formal negotiation and conditional compromise
- passive/process description
- relative/detail expansion
- inference from evidence at a practical B2 level
- concession and disagreement
- viewpoint summarisation
- a community-facing capstone

These are legitimate B2 targets. Counterfactuals, qualified stance, negotiation, connected causal explanation and summarising viewpoints may remain in B2 when tasks stay concrete enough and do not demand C1-style epistemic/argument architecture.

## C1 boundary derived from the current next-level curriculum

The current C1 curriculum explicitly raises the ceiling to repeated control of: calibrated evidential distance and confidence; claim/warrant/counterpoint/scope argument architecture; faithful reporting that distinguishes quotation, paraphrase, inference and interpretation; dense qualification; and more abstract analytical discourse. B2 must therefore not require sustained productive control of those C1 systems.

B2 boundary rule for expansion:

1. Evidence/inference tasks may ask learners to identify clues, make a plausible inference and qualify it with ordinary B2 probability language. They must not require systematic source-quality calibration, explicit epistemic distance, competing explanatory models, or confidence-strength calibration as a discourse system.
2. Opinion/argument tasks may require a clear position, reasons, examples, advantages/disadvantages and response to another view. They must not require explicit warrant analysis, scope-of-validity architecture, counterexample theory or formal claim-evidence critique.
3. Mediation may summarise and combine a small number of accessible viewpoints for a practical decision. It must not require high-density multi-source synthesis with source-position preservation and interpretive caveats characteristic of C1.
4. Register work may shift between ordinary informal, neutral and routine formal contexts. It must not require fine-grained rhetorical persona/style manipulation.

## Structural/depth finding

The legacy B2 units are two-lesson production files. B2 therefore lacks repeated independent output across the level even where recognition/dialogue content is good. Existing units should receive a genuine third lesson during retrofit, with open writing, personalised speaking, mediation, negotiation, problem solving or synthesis appropriate to B2. A third lesson must not be another recognition drill.

The observed 10-unit tranche is far below the planning breadth safeguard (~45), but the number itself is not the reason for incompleteness. The substantive issue is sparse domain coverage and insufficient repeated production/recycling.

## Priority coverage gaps for expansion

### High priority

- extended but accessible reading/listening: identify main line, supporting detail, speaker attitude and implied practical conclusion
- independent connected writing: complaint/response, proposal, review, recommendation, explanatory message and short discursive text
- independent speaking: sustained explanation, comparison, recommendation and experience-based argument with follow-up questions
- practical mediation: relay and condense information from notices, messages, short articles, instructions or two viewpoints for another person
- online interaction: threaded discussion, clarification, repair, polite challenge, convergence and decision recording
- register/pragmatics: neutral vs routine-formal requests, complaints, disagreement, softening and follow-up
- negotiation/problem solving: constraints, trade-offs, compromise, contingency and agreed action
- narrative/reporting at B2: coherent event/account reporting with relevance and viewpoint, without C1 source-distance analysis
- review/recycling clusters that force earlier grammar/discourse resources to reappear in new domains
- a later exit capstone integrating reception, mediation, interaction and independent production; the current community-forum capstone should be retained as an interim integration task rather than treated as proof of level completion

### Domain breadth to add

Work/study coordination; housing and local services; travel disruption; consumer/service escalation; health/wellbeing communication without specialist medical claims; community participation; media/technology use; culture/events/reviews; environment and everyday policy choices; learning strategies; workplace collaboration; public-facing information and routine institutional communication.

## First justified expansion chunk

The next B2 implementation chunk should be variable-sized and coverage-led. Start with 4 units that fill different high-priority gaps rather than duplicating the legacy grammar list:

1. `workplace-proposal-and-feedback` — receive a short proposal + colleague reactions; compare constraints; write/speak a reasoned recommendation; routine-formal register.
2. `travel-disruption-resolution` — combine timetable/service information; negotiate alternatives; mediate the agreed plan to another traveller; practical inference only.
3. `review-and-recommendation` — extract criteria from two accessible reviews; distinguish fact/opinion; produce a balanced recommendation with examples, without C1 source-calibration.
4. `online-group-decision` — threaded interaction with clarification, polite challenge, compromise and a concise decision summary.

Each unit should contain 3 substantive lessons. Lesson 3 must include independently evaluated output (open writing plus personalised speaking and/or mediation/interaction) with an explicit rubric.

## QA required before accepting expansion

- add/extend a cumulative Turkish B2↔C1 educational boundary validator/review that checks task semantics as well as curated linguistic patterns; do not ban ordinary lexical items blindly
- validate all legacy and new B2 production, including no repeated normalised dialogue pair in consecutive lessons
- verify all B2 units have substantive third-lesson output after retrofit
- cumulative cast/character rotation validation
- MySQL dry-run, canonical counts and idempotency from current production/specs rather than fixed totals
- delay paid B2 audio until the structural expansion chunk/level is stable

## Completion decision

Turkish B2 is **not breadth-complete**. Preserve the current 10 units, retrofit their missing production depth, and expand from demonstrated skill/domain gaps. Do not mark complete at any round unit count; reassess cumulative reception, production, interaction, mediation, pragmatics/register, recycling and capstone evidence after each stable expansion tranche.
