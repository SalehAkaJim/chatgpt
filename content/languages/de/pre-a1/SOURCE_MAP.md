Status: source mapping

This file maps the provisional Pre-A1 lesson plan to concrete source locations. It is a planning/provenance layer only. It does **not** authorize invented German text. A lesson remains `draft` until its exact source dialogue/lines are extracted, translated to Persian, assigned to characters, and QA-checked.

## Source abbreviations

- **HS** — DLI/FSI German Headstart, public-domain U.S. Government material.
- **BASIC** — FSI German Basic Course, public-domain U.S. Government material.
- **FAST** — FSI German FAST, public-domain U.S. Government material.
- **PI** — FSI German Programmed Introduction, public-domain U.S. Government material. Used as a pronunciation/drill fallback when the mapped situational source does not provide enough staged practice.

## Source facts verified during mapping

- Headstart Module I Unit 2 explicitly trains asking about common-place locations, asking what something is called in German, and courtesy expressions. It also contains conversations with explicit male/female speaker labels and picture-supported place vocabulary.
- Headstart Module III explicitly focuses on ordering a meal, common food/beverage items, menu reading, waiter/waitress questions and prices.
- Headstart Module IV covers telling time and days of the week.
- Headstart Module V covers hotel/taxi situations, duration/time expressions and calendar dates; its hotel material contains registration-related work.
- Headstart Module VI covers shopping. Its Unit 1 source role is a **female sales clerk**, so the app character must be female for those turns.
- Headstart Module VII covers telephone/post-office situations and includes telephone numbers.
- Headstart Module VIII covers visiting a German family, introductions and getting-acquainted topics, including residence.
- FAST Lesson 2 contains a real registration form with name, profession, birth date/place, nationality, residence and street fields.
- FAST Lesson 4 contains age questions in its ticket-counter variant material.
- BASIC Units 2 and 4 provide compact source-backed greetings, name/origin and family/personal-information exchanges.

## Gate before lesson content is created

A mapped lesson may move from `draft` to `source_checked` only after:

1. the exact target-language dialogue/lines have been copied from the mapped source;
2. the exact locator/page/section is saved in provenance;
3. Persian translation has been produced;
4. speaker identity/gender evidence has been checked;
5. the extracted content is still appropriate for Pre-A1 when isolated from its source context;
6. the activity count and order are derived from the usable material, not from a fixed template.

The provisional count remains 30 until all thirty mappings survive extraction. Lessons may merge or split if the source material makes that educationally necessary.
