# Turkish (tr-TR) cumulative CEFR coverage matrix

This matrix is the level-close source of truth for the Persian→Istanbul Turkish course. A 10-unit production batch is an atomic chunk only; directory presence or one completed batch never closes a CEFR level.

## Pre-A1

Current Batch 1 is a deliberately compact zero-learner foundation. It covers greetings/farewells, names, numbers 0–10, Turkish alphabet contrasts including `i/ı`, first sound-letter mappings, simple wellbeing, first objects, polite social chunks, survival repair language, and a first integrated conversation. This is appropriate compact Pre-A1 coverage because its purpose is readiness for A1 rather than broad independent language use.

## A1

### Batch 1 — survival foundations

Identity/person endings and `değil`; demonstratives/plural; close family possession; locative + `var/yok`; present progressive; clock time and simple arrangements; café requests; prices/quantities; dative directions; first-day survival capstone.

### Batch 2 — everyday tense/case expansion

Aorist routines; accusative specific object; ablative origin; `-(y)Abil` ability/polite requests; definite past `-DI`; weather/simple predicates; symptoms and basic imperative advice; `önce/sonra`; future `-(y)AcAk`; communication repair and cumulative everyday capstone.

10. a1-community-capstone — integrated A1 transfer across social, schedule, shopping and transport tasks
