# German B2 Coverage Map

Updated: 2026-09-13

## Purpose

German B2 is designed for Persian-speaking learners who have completed the German B1 course. The level is not assigned a fixed unit count in advance. Work proceeds in batches of 1-10 units, and the level closes only after a cumulative coverage/gap audit shows that the required B2 communicative and language-specific capabilities are represented.

## B2 exit capabilities

By the end of B2, the learner should be able to:

- follow and produce extended connected speech on familiar and moderately abstract topics;
- build a clear argument, support claims with reasons/examples/evidence, and respond to counterarguments;
- express degrees of certainty, reservation, concession and nuance rather than only yes/no positions;
- negotiate, compromise, clarify responsibilities and handle disagreement in work/social settings;
- summarize, compare and relay information from multiple sources without copying wording;
- interpret trends, quantities and simple data and explain what they may or may not imply;
- participate in structured discussions about society, technology, environment, education and work;
- write connected practical/formal messages with an appropriate register and clear structure;
- understand indirect or compressed formulations common in notices, reports, media and workplace communication;
- reformulate ideas when vocabulary is missing and keep interaction moving naturally.

## Language-specific German progression

B2 grammar is selected for communicative value rather than taught as an isolated checklist. Coverage should include, across the level:

- richer connector systems: `dennoch`, `hingegen`, `folglich`, `somit`, `insofern`, `sofern`, `während`, `wohingegen`;
- concession and contrast: `obwohl`, `auch wenn`, `selbst wenn`, `zwar ... aber`, `einerseits ... andererseits`;
- advanced condition/hypothesis with Konjunktiv II, including past counterfactuals;
- indirect speech and reported information, including practical use of Konjunktiv I where natural;
- passive voice across tenses plus common passive alternatives (`man`, `sich lassen`, `sein + zu + Infinitiv` where appropriate);
- nominalization and verb-noun style common in formal/workplace German;
- participial and expanded adjective phrases when useful for comprehension;
- relative clauses with prepositions and selected genitive relatives;
- verb-preposition combinations and pronominal adverbs (`darauf`, `womit`, `woran`, etc.);
- stance/evidential language for certainty, probability, source distance and cautious claims;
- information structure and word order in longer subordinate-clause chains;
- register control: neutral, polite/formal, collaborative and cautiously critical language.

## Coverage domains

The level should eventually cover these capability clusters. Their order and exact number of units may change after audits.

1. Argumentation, evidence and counterarguments
2. Nuanced agreement, disagreement and concession
3. Extended explanations, presentations and Q&A
4. Negotiation, compromise and conflict resolution
5. Meetings, decisions, responsibilities and follow-up
6. Formal correspondence, requests and tactful escalation
7. Summarizing and comparing multiple information sources
8. Media literacy, claims, uncertainty and source evaluation
9. Trends, data, quantities and interpretation
10. Society, community and civic discussion
11. Technology, digital change and consequences
12. Environment, sustainability and trade-offs
13. Education, learning strategies and institutional communication
14. Work, professional development and organizational change
15. Consumer/service disputes with nuanced problem solving
16. Housing, mobility and urban-life decisions at a more abstract level
17. Health/well-being communication without operational medical advice
18. Culture, identity, norms and perspectives
19. Planning under uncertainty, risk and alternatives
20. Integrated B2 capstone scenarios combining several skills

## Batch 1 focus

Batch 1 establishes the core B2 discourse layer before domain expansion:

1. `Argumentieren & Belege nennen`
2. `Nuanciert zustimmen & widersprechen`
3. `Präsentieren & Rückfragen beantworten`
4. `Verhandeln & Kompromisse finden`
5. `Besprechungen & Entscheidungen`
6. `Formelle Nachrichten & Eskalation`
7. `Quellen zusammenfassen & vergleichen`
8. `Medien, Behauptungen & Unsicherheit`
9. `Daten, Trends & Entwicklungen`
10. `Gesellschaftliche Themen diskutieren`

## Quality rules

- 3 lessons per unit.
- 6 lexical items, 6 target utterances, 3 grammar points, 3 dialogues, 15 exercises per unit under the current production schema.
- Persian translations must be natural and meaning-faithful.
- German must sound native/idiomatic at B2 and avoid artificial textbook stiffness.
- Exact two-character dialogue pairs may not repeat consecutively across lesson boundaries.
- Avoid topic duplication that only changes nouns while keeping the same communicative task.
- Medical, legal and financial scenarios remain language-learning contexts, not operational advice.
- Each later batch must be checked cumulatively against this map.
- Near the end of B2, run a fresh gap audit; add only genuinely missing capabilities.
- The final B2 batch may contain fewer than 10 units.
